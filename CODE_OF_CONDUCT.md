# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Focus criticism on behavior and code. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sharing private information without permission and repeated personal attacks are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove disruptive material or restrict participation to keep the project usable. Send conduct concerns privately to abuzart1999@gmail.com, including links or evidence you are comfortable sharing. Reports are handled privately where possible; do not publish other people's personal details in issues.
